# v05: discreet end vent bands

User rejected the cage-like vent layout and asked for smaller slots nearer the top/bottom, and an explanation of the square opening.

- Slots reduced from 18 x 2.4 to 10 x 1.4 mm. Two rows retained per half, now 31.5 and 34.5 mm from the seam (6.5 and 3.5 mm from the exterior end). Three slots per row/side centered -20,0,20 mm; 24 per half. Center walls stay closed.
- USB-C aperture remains functional for charging without opening the firm latch. The 12 x 6 mm opening now has R2 corners. Charger location unchanged.
- R2.5 exterior, 76 mm outer bounds, thermal pads, snaps and internal component positions retained.
- Generator checks passed: 0 boundary edges, connected halves, no nominal collisions, 1.2 mm thermal pads.
- Updated both G-codes and embedded-profile 3MFs with the existing XLIS 0.4 / Generic PLA / 0.20 STRUCTURAL configuration. Each 3MF was re-sliced successfully. Automatic snug supports enabled; no physical print test.
- Bottom estimate 4h 6m 41s / 97.27 g; lid 3h 11m 16s / 85.00 g including supports/brim.
- New front/rear geometry overview derived from actual generator mesh. Earlier support screenshots remain with v04; they do not depict v05.
- Reduced vent area follows visual intent; airflow/cooling performance remains untested.
