# Pneumaatika kuubik v01 — print notes

## Request and decisions, 2026-11-10

New, separate one-piece hollow cube for a pneumatic membrane (balloon).
Reference exterior 80 mm / R8; three equal apertures on each of six faces.
The user explicitly approved adjusting aperture diameter and placement while
retaining 80 mm / R8, because the reference's Ø35 / 35 mm pitch leaves zero web.

Selected: Ø27 mm throats, R1.6 blends on BOTH sides, 4 mm wall, minimum face web
between rounded mouths 3.2 mm. No holders, seam, thermal pads or closure.

## Digital result — awaiting physical test

Selected edge-down 45° orientation, 0.15 mm PLA / XL 0.4 mm. Only removable
external startup Snug supports: 1.27 g estimated, ending at Z=5.8 mm. No support
path samples inside the cavity. Total 108.95 g / 8h34m47s estimated.

The model and serialized STL checks pass; final embedded-profile 3MF was sliced
and checked. Printed surface quality, support removal and balloon durability
have not been physically verified. Smooth and inspect both sides of every
aperture before inserting a membrane. No operating pressure is specified.
